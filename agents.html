What makes this multi-agent?

Three separate StudentAgent instances own their profiles. A coordinator proposes plans and receives ballots. It selects a feasible plan by the smallest longest walk, then total walking, venue preferences and start time. No agent can waive a hard constraint or confirm for its student.
